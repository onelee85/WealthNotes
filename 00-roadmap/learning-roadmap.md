# 学习路线

这条路线从家庭财务事实出发，依次理解风险、组合、工具、纪律和评价方法。每一阶段完成后再进入下一阶段；专题学习按现实问题需要穿插进行。

## Stage 1 — Family Finance

课程：

1. [Balance Sheet](../01-family-finance/01-balance-sheet.md)
2. [Cash Flow](../01-family-finance/02-cash-flow.md)
3. [Emergency Fund](../01-family-finance/03-emergency-fund.md)
4. [Financial Goals](../01-family-finance/04-financial-goals.md)
5. [Time Horizon](../01-family-finance/05-time-horizon.md)
6. [Money Buckets](../01-family-finance/06-money-buckets.md)

学习目标：

> 看清家庭的钱在哪里、有什么用途、什么时候需要。

## Stage 2 — Risk & Return

课程：

1. [Return & Compounding](../02-risk-and-return/01-return-and-compounding.md)
2. [Volatility](../02-risk-and-return/02-volatility.md)
3. [Drawdown](../02-risk-and-return/03-drawdown.md)
4. [Risk Capacity](../02-risk-and-return/04-risk-capacity.md)
5. [Risk Tolerance](../02-risk-and-return/05-risk-tolerance.md)
6. [Common Risks](../02-risk-and-return/06-common-risks.md)

学习目标：

> 理解收益背后的风险，并建立正确的风险认知。

## Stage 3 — Asset Allocation

课程：

1. [Asset Classes](../03-asset-allocation/01-asset-classes.md)
2. [Diversification](../03-asset-allocation/02-diversification.md)
3. [Asset Allocation](../03-asset-allocation/03-asset-allocation.md)
4. [Rebalancing](../03-asset-allocation/04-rebalancing.md)

学习目标：

> 从“买什么产品”升级为“家庭资产应该如何组合”。

## Stage 4 — Investment Products

课程：

1. [Mutual Funds](../04-investment-products/01-mutual-funds.md)
2. [Index Funds](../04-investment-products/02-index-funds.md)
3. [ETF](../04-investment-products/03-etf.md)
4. [Active Funds](../04-investment-products/04-active-funds.md)
5. [Bonds](../04-investment-products/05-bonds.md)
6. [Bank Wealth Products](../04-investment-products/06-bank-wealth-products.md)
7. [Gold](../04-investment-products/07-gold.md)

学习目标：

> 理解不同投资工具的风险来源和组合角色。

## Stage 5 — Investment Discipline

课程：

1. [DCA](../05-investment-discipline/01-dca.md)
2. [Buy Decisions](../05-investment-discipline/02-buy-decisions.md)
3. [Sell Decisions](../05-investment-discipline/03-sell-decisions.md)
4. [Behavioral Finance](../05-investment-discipline/04-behavioral-finance.md)
5. [Common Mistakes](../05-investment-discipline/05-common-mistakes.md)

学习目标：

> 减少临时决策、追涨杀跌和情绪化操作。

## Stage 6 — Performance

课程：

1. [Return Evaluation](../06-performance/01-return-evaluation.md)
2. [Benchmark](../06-performance/02-benchmark.md)
3. [XIRR](../06-performance/03-xirr.md)
4. [Performance Attribution](../06-performance/04-performance-attribution.md)
5. [Investment Review](../06-performance/05-investment-review.md)

学习目标：

> 正确判断投资结果，而不是只看“赚了还是亏了”。

## 按需学习区

`07-topics` 不属于固定主线。只有当现实投资问题需要理解通胀、利率、货币政策或汇率时，再进入相应专题。
